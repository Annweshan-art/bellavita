The Floral Inspiration

Bellavita Blush was born from the breathtaking sight of early morning jasmine flowers blooming under the warm Italian sun. As soft breezes flutter across garden terraces, the white petals release their purest, most intoxicating aroma.

“We envisioned a fragrance alive with sunlight and gentle winds — timeless in poise, but constantly dancing with nature.”

— Atelier Bellavita Master Nose

Flacon Architecture

Crafted from optical-grade heavy glass, the rectangular silhouette features crisp beveled borders, highlighting the glowing blush nectar inside. Capped with an architectural cylindrical chrome crown that catches ambient sunbeams from every angle.
